# Code of Conduct

MoonShine V follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
It applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews and any other project space,
and to people representing the project elsewhere.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and considerate, including when you disagree.
- Give and accept constructive feedback about the work, not the person.
- Assume good intent and help newcomers find their way.
- Keep other people's personal data, credentials and private conversations private.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, threats or discriminatory language or imagery.
- Sexualized language or attention, and unwelcome personal contact.
- Publishing someone's private information without their explicit permission.
- Trolling, deliberate disruption, or sustained off-topic or bad-faith arguments.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct problems privately to the maintainers at
[thecutcode@gmail.com](mailto:thecutcode@gmail.com). Do not use this address for
security vulnerabilities; follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.

Maintainers review every report, keep the reporter's identity confidential and may
edit or remove contributions, or warn, temporarily ban or permanently ban a
participant, following the Contributor Covenant enforcement guidelines.
